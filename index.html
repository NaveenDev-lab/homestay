The Villa

A Home, Only More So.

Jeevika Haven isn't a room you rent — it's an entire villa you inherit for a few days. Four AC bedrooms, a sunlit living hall, a fully equipped kitchen, and a lawn that runs into the trees.

Every door is yours, every hour unhurried. Mornings begin with filter coffee on the porch; evenings end by firelight, pool lights glowing against the dark.
